# Grid aggregate — `grid-2026-06-11`

Multi-seed aggregation of the scale-up grid. Generated by
`python -m analysis.aggregate_seeds grid-2026-06-11` (Supabase
`experiment_summaries`), 2026-06-17.

- **Run group:** `grid-2026-06-11`
- **Coverage:** 3 tickers (AAPL, JPM, SPY) × 5 seeds = 15 summary rows, complete.
- **Eval:** walk-forward, rolling 1-step; per-seed Diebold–Mariano, reported as a
  "k of N seeds significant" distribution (Workstream C decision).
- **Sanity check:** the seed-invariant classical models (`naive`, `sarimax`) show
  ±0.00000 std across seeds, exactly as expected.

## Headline

**SARIMAX has the lowest MAE on all three tickers and beats every neural model
5/5 seeds under Diebold–Mariano.** Every model in turn beats `naive` 5/5, so the
networks do learn signal — they are simply edged out by the classical baseline.
Directional accuracy sits at chance (0.45–0.50) for all models including SARIMAX,
so the MAE advantage is a magnitude/calibration win, not a directional one.

Among the neural models the pairwise DM comparisons are almost all 0/5 — they are
statistically indistinguishable from one another. The only flicker of separation
is `lstm` vs `cfc` (2/5 on AAPL and JPM, 0/5 on SPY), which is not robust.

The `_raw` input group consistently yields slightly lower MAE than `_transformed`
for the liquid networks (e.g. AAPL `ltc_raw` 0.01394 vs `ltc_transformed` 0.01544).
